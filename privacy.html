Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This page explains what information is collected when you visit Filtered Thoughts (blog.nedworldwide.com), how it's used, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Filtered Thoughts is a blog run by Ned (Ned Worldwide) in San Antonio, Texas, with posts from Ned and guest contributors. When this policy says "we," it means the blog.

Information we collect

We don't ask you to create an account, and the blog has no sign-up forms or comments. We don't collect your name, email or other personal details unless you contact us directly.

Like most websites, some information is collected automatically when you visit:

  • Visit statistics. We use a simple analytics script (Ned Traffic) to count page views and see which pages and links are popular. It records things like the page you viewed, the site that referred you, your browser and device type, and the time of your visit.
  • Server logs. The blog is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical information such as your IP address for security and to keep the service running. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads on this blog. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you may be shown a consent message asking whether you agree to cookies being used for ads.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. This blog itself doesn't need cookies to work, but our advertising partners and embedded content may set them. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some ads may become less relevant if you do.

Embedded content and outside services

  • YouTube videos. Some posts include embedded YouTube videos. YouTube (Google) may collect data and set cookies when you play them.
  • Google Fonts. Our pages load fonts from Google, which receives your IP address to deliver them.
  • Affiliate links. Some posts include Amazon affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. If you click one, Amazon may use cookies to track the referral.
  • Links to other sites. We link to other websites, including our own projects. Their privacy practices are their own.

How we use information

We use visit statistics to understand what readers find useful and to improve the blog. We don't sell your personal information.

Children's privacy

This blog isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can manage ad personalization through Google's Ads Settings, block cookies in your browser, or use a browser or extension that limits tracking. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access or delete information about you; contact us and we'll help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
