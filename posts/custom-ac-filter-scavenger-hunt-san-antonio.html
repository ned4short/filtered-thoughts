Can’t Find Your AC Filter Size? Let’s End the Scavenger Hunt

Some errands should be simple. Buying an AC filter shouldn’t turn into a three-store tour of San Antonio with a dusty rectangle riding shotgun.

If you’ve ever found a shelf full of filters that were almost your size, you know the frustration. I put together a new guide on the Ned Air Filters website for homeowners and businesses dealing with rare or custom AC filter sizes.

It covers the difference between printed and actual dimensions, what to measure before ordering, and the information to include when requesting a quote. There are also answers to common questions about fit and replacement schedules.

A tape measure is a useful shopping companion. A filter that has to be folded like a taco to fit your return grille is a reason to double-check the size.
