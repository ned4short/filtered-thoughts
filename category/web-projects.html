KitReel: Turn a Full Soccer Game Into Your Kid's Highlight Reel

KitReel is a free tool that turns a long soccer game video into a highlight reel of one player. Upload an MP4 (up to 2 GB, up to 60 minutes), pick the team color, tag your player, and download their reel. No account, and your video stays on your device.

RoutePilot: A Free Route Planner for Multiple Addresses (Up to 50 Stops)

RoutePilot is a free route planner and optimizer for multiple addresses. Paste or upload up to 50 stops, pick how your route ends, and get the fastest order to drive them. No account needed.
